wasm-satsview
=============

A GNSS satellite sky-view control with a Rust/WebAssembly rendering core and
JavaScript/TypeScript entrypoints. The default view is north-up: azimuth increases
clockwise, the horizon is at the perimeter, and the zenith is at the center.
